Empirical Calibration Finding:
Isotonic regression overfit the 2023 validation distribution and increased test-set ECE across all models ($0.0092 \to 0.0137$ in HGB 31; $0.0150 \to 0.0171$ in LightGBM 39). Parametric Platt scaling (logistic on log-odds) regularized estimates, successfully reducing test-set ECE. Brier score refinement was consistent across multimodal feature additions, demonstrating that Brier improvement is mathematically distinct from ECE reduction.

5. Data Provenance & Observational Caveats

Methodological Constraints and Remote-Sensing Boundary Conditions

1. Single DEM Provenance: Topographic elevation, canonical 3×3 Horn slope, and 8-neighbor Riley TRI are derived from the NOAA ETOPO 2022 Global Relief Model (v1, 15 arc-second grid), which integrates NASA SRTM v3.0 land elevation, resampled to 0.10° (~11.1 km) grid centroids across 93,611 sovereign grid cells.

2. Retrospective 1:1 Case-Control Sampling: The 131,000-sample dataset employs a balanced case-control retrospective design ($P(Y=1) = 0.50$). Model outputs estimate sample posterior probabilities under equal class priors, not nationwide unconditioned incidence (which is $< 0.05\%$ per cell daily).

3. Negative Label Interpretation ($Y=0$): A negative observation denotes the absence of confirmed VIIRS thermal anomaly detection during orbital overpasses (which may occur due to cloud cover, heavy smoke attenuation, or overpass timing), rather than guaranteed physical non-existence of ground combustion.

4. Epistemic Uncertainty Limitation: Spearman correlation between Monte Carlo Dropout standard deviation and empirical absolute error was $r_s = -0.1355$ ($p < 0.001$). Dropout variance alone does not provide a monotonic error detector without spatial distance-to-support bounds.
